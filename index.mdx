Consensus is an AI search engine for research. It searches **over 200 million peer-reviewed papers** and returns ranked results with grounded, DOI-linked citations and AI summaries, so you can find, understand, and cite the evidence without wading through search engines built for the open web.

This section covers the Consensus web app: how to search, read and analyze results, and organize your library.
