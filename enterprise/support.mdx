Enterprise plans are set up with our team so your limits, access, and security fit your organization. Here is how to get started and where to get help.

## Get Enterprise access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Talk to sales">
    Tell us what you are building through the [API and Enterprise page](https://consensus.app/home/api/) or the [contact form](https://consensus.app/home/contact/). Share your expected volume, the fields you need (full text, DOI), and any auth requirements such as SSO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope your plan">
    We size your custom rate limits and papers per search, confirm full-text and DOI access, and plan SSO and organization-scoped keys with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provision and onboard">
    We provision your organization-scoped API keys and MCP access, then help your team integrate and go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Onboarding

Onboarding gets your team from a signed plan to a working integration. At a high level it covers:

- Provisioning organization-scoped API keys and connecting the [MCP server](/mcp/authentication).
- Setting your custom rate limits and papers per search.
- Turning on full-text excerpts and DOI access, and confirming the [fields](/enterprise/full-text-and-fields) your workload needs.
- Configuring single sign-on (SSO) for your organization.

The exact steps depend on your setup and are planned with you during scoping.

## SLAs and contract terms

Service level agreements, uptime commitments, security terms, and pricing are part of your Enterprise agreement and are tailored to your organization. For current terms, contact [sales](https://consensus.app/home/api/).
