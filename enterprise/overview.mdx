Enterprise is for teams running Consensus in production or at research scale. It builds on everything in the [REST API](/api-reference/overview) and [MCP server](/mcp/authentication), then raises the limits and unlocks the paper data that larger workloads need.

## What Enterprise adds

- **Custom rate limits and papers per search.** Standard plans return up to 20 papers per search. Enterprise sets your rate limits and papers per search to match your workload. See [Rate limits and tiers](/rate-limits-and-tiers).
- **Full-text access.** Request query-relevant excerpts from each paper's full text with `include_full_text_chunks`, not just abstracts. See [Full text and advanced fields](/enterprise/full-text-and-fields).
- **DOI and advanced fields.** Responses include each paper's DOI plus advanced access to paper metadata. See [Full text and advanced fields](/enterprise/full-text-and-fields).
- **SSO and enterprise authentication.** Organization-scoped API keys and MCP OAuth 2.1, with single sign-on (SSO) for your organization. See [Security and access](/enterprise/security).
- **Dedicated support.** Priority support and hands-on onboarding for your team. See [Getting access and support](/enterprise/support).

<Note>
  Monthly search quotas vary by plan. See [pricing](https://consensus.app/pricing) for current quotas, and [Rate limits and tiers](/rate-limits-and-tiers) for papers-per-search caps by tier.
</Note>
