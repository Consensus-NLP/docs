The Consensus MCP server exposes two tools. Every client gets **search**. ChatGPT additionally gets **fetch**.

## search

Searches over 200 million peer-reviewed papers and returns ranked results with metadata. Your LLM host calls this tool whenever it needs research evidence.

### Parameters

<ParamField query="query" type="string" required>
  The research question or topic to search for.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year_min" type="integer">
  Earliest publication year to include (inclusive).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year_max" type="integer">
  Latest publication year to include (inclusive).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="study_types" type="string[]">
  Restrict to specific study designs. Allowed values: `rct`, `meta-analysis`, `systematic review`, `literature review`, `case report`, `non-rct experimental`, `non-rct observational study`, `non-rct in vitro`, `animal`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sjr_max" type="integer">
  Maximum SJR journal quartile to include, where `1` is Q1 (highest quality) and `4` is Q4. Set `1` to return Q1 journals only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="human" type="boolean">
  Restrict to human studies only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sample_size_min" type="integer">
  Minimum study sample size.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="medical_mode" type="boolean">
  Restrict to top medical journals and clinical guidelines.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="exclude_preprints" type="boolean">
  Exclude preprints and return peer-reviewed papers only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="duration_min" type="integer">
  Minimum study duration in days.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="duration_max" type="integer">
  Maximum study duration in days.
</ParamField>

### Response

Each result includes:

<ResponseField name="title" type="string">
  Paper title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="authors" type="string[]">
  Author names.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="abstract" type="string">
  Paper abstract.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="journal" type="string">
  Journal name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="year" type="integer">
  Publication year.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="citation_count" type="integer">
  Number of times the paper has been cited.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="url" type="string">
  Link to the paper.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="study_type" type="string">
  Study design classification. Available on Pro, Deep, and Teams plans.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="takeaway" type="string">
  One-sentence AI summary of the paper's finding. Available on Pro, Deep, and Teams plans.
</ResponseField>

<Note>
  `study_type` and `takeaway` are added on Pro and above. Anonymous and Free results include the core fields only. See [Authentication](/mcp/authentication) for how your plan is determined.
</Note>

## fetch

Retrieves the full details of a single paper by its id.

### Parameters

<ParamField query="id" type="string" required>
  The 32-character paper id returned by `search`.
</ParamField>

### Response

Returns the full record for the paper, including its title, authors, abstract, journal, year, citation count, and URL.

<Note>
  `fetch` is available in ChatGPT only. Other clients use `search`, which returns the same metadata for every result.
</Note>
