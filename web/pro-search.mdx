Pro Search goes a step beyond a ranked list. It analyzes up to 20 papers and writes a thoughtful summary that connects findings across them, so you see not just which papers are relevant but what they say together. Pro Search is powered by the [Research Agent](/web/research-agent) and supports [Threads](/web/threads) for follow-up questions.

## When to use it

Choose Pro Search when a fast scan is not enough and you want the evidence synthesized:

- You have a focused question and want the findings pulled together, not just listed.
- You want to understand where papers agree and where they diverge.
- You are drafting or fact-checking a claim and need it grounded in several papers at once.
- You expect to ask follow-up questions and want to keep the context with [Threads](/web/threads).

## How it works

Behind the scenes, the [Research Agent](/web/research-agent) plans and runs multiple sub-searches, uses tools such as citation crawling and author search, applies academic filters, and reads and compares papers before writing its summary. The result is a connected synthesis of up to 20 papers with grounded, cited claims.

The analysis can also render diagrams, including [Mermaid diagrams](/web/diagrams-in-analysis), to visualize a process or a comparison in the evidence.

## Keep going with Threads

After a Pro Search, you do not have to start over to dig deeper. [Threads](/web/threads) let you ask follow-up questions within the same search while keeping the context of your question and the papers already found.

## How it differs from Quick and Deep

- **[Quick Search](/web/quick-search)** is faster and returns ranked papers with one-sentence takeaways, without a written synthesis.
- **[Deep review](/web/deep-review)** goes broader still: it breaks a complex question into sub-questions and reviews up to 50 papers into a structured literature review.
