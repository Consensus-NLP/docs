Chat with full text lets you open a conversation about a single paper, grounded in its **full text** rather than just the abstract. Ask targeted questions about that study and get answers drawn from the paper itself.

<Info>
  This works with papers that are **in the Consensus corpus**. To ask questions about a document of your own, such as a PDF that is not in the corpus, use [Upload and chat with your documents](/web/upload-and-chat) instead.
</Info>

## When to use it

Reach for Chat with full text when the abstract is not enough and you have specific questions about one paper, for example:

- "What was the sample size, and how were participants recruited?"
- "What limitations do the authors acknowledge?"
- "How did they measure the primary outcome?"
- "What does the discussion conclude about real-world application?"

Because the conversation is grounded in the paper's full text, the answers point back to what the study actually reports, not to the wider literature.

## Start a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a paper from your results">
    Find a paper in your search results that you want to dig into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting with its full text">
    Open the chat for that paper to begin a conversation grounded in its full text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask targeted questions">
    Ask about methods, results, limitations, or anything specific to that study. Follow up to go deeper on any answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Answers are AI summaries of the paper and can miss nuance. For anything high-stakes, confirm against the passage in the paper itself.
</Warning>

## Chat with full text vs upload and chat

Both give you a grounded conversation about one paper. The difference is whose document it is:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat with full text" icon="comments">
    Ask questions about a paper that is already **in the Consensus corpus**. Nothing to upload.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upload and chat" icon="file-arrow-up" href="/web/upload-and-chat">
    Bring your **own PDF** and chat with it the same way, even if it is not in the corpus.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
