description: "Share a collection with others so they can see the papers you have gathered on a topic."
---

A collection is a curated set of papers, which makes it a convenient thing to hand to someone else. Share a collection to give a colleague, a co-author, or a study group the papers you have gathered on a topic, without sending them one link at a time.

## How sharing works

Build a [collection](/web/collections) around a project, a research question, or a manuscript, then share it. The people you share it with can see the papers in the collection, so a shared collection works as a ready-made reading list or a common evidence base for a group.

<Tip>
  Organize the collection before you share it. A focused, well-named collection is easier for others to make sense of than a long, mixed list.
</Tip>
