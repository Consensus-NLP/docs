My Library is where the papers and searches you choose to keep live. Save a paper you want to read later, or save an entire search so you can pick the thread back up without running it again.

<Note>
  My Library holds what you deliberately save. It is separate from your [search history](/web/search-history), which records your past searches automatically. Use the library for the papers and searches worth keeping.
</Note>

## What you can save

- **Individual papers.** Save any paper from your results to keep it in your library and read, cite, or organize it later.
- **Whole searches.** Save a search to return to the question and its ranked results later, instead of running it from scratch.

## How saving works

When a paper or search is worth keeping, **save** it. Saved papers and saved searches both land in My Library, so everything you have set aside sits in one place.

From there you can group saved papers into [collections](/web/collections) built around a project, a research question, or a manuscript.

## Where to find saved items

Open **My Library** to see everything you have saved. A saved search takes you back to the original question and its results; a saved paper is ready to read, cite, or add to a collection.
