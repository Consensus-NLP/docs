Every paper in Consensus is ready to cite. Generate a formatted citation in the style your work requires, then copy it into your document or send it straight to your reference manager.

## Citation styles

You can generate a citation in any of these styles:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="APA" icon="quote-left">

  </Card>
  <Card title="MLA" icon="quote-left">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Chicago" icon="quote-left">
    Common in history and some social sciences.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vancouver" icon="quote-left">
    Common in medicine and the life sciences.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create a citation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the paper you want to cite">
    Find the paper in your results or your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a citation style">
    Pick APA, MLA, Chicago, or Vancouver to match your field or your publication's requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the citation">
    Copy the formatted citation and paste it into your manuscript, notes, or bibliography.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Generated citations draw on the paper's indexed metadata. Give any citation a quick check against your target style before you submit, especially for edge cases like many authors or missing fields.
</Tip>

## Export to a reference manager

Rather than copy citations by hand, you can export papers to a reference manager and keep your bibliography in one place. Consensus exports to:

- **EndNote**
- **Zotero**
- **Paperpile**
