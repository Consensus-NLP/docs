---
title: "Full-text access with LibKey"
description: "Connect your institution's LibKey subscription for one-click full-text access to paywalled papers."
---

Consensus integrates with LibKey so that if you are covered by an institution's site license, you get one-click access to the full text of paywalled papers through your library's subscriptions. Consensus rolled out LibKey access for the 2025-26 academic year.

## Turn on LibKey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Settings">
    Go to your **Settings** page in Consensus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your institution">
    Choose your institution to connect its LibKey subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open papers through your library">
    Once connected, paywalled papers route you to your university's access page, where your library subscription unlocks the full text in one click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  LibKey is an institutional library subscription, not licensed to individuals. If you do not see your institution, or cannot unlock papers, check with your library about availability.
</Warning>

<Note>
  For a walkthrough of institutional access, see [Getting started with Consensus university access](https://consensus.app/home/blog/getting-started-with-consensus-university-access/).
</Note>
