---
title: "Upload and chat with your documents"
description: "Upload your own PDF and chat with it, asking targeted questions that are answered from the document you provided."
---

Upload and chat lets you bring your **own PDF** into Consensus and hold a conversation grounded in it. Ask targeted questions and get answers drawn from the document you uploaded.

<Info>
  This is for documents you supply, such as a paper that is not in the corpus, a preprint, a report, or your own draft. To ask questions about a paper that is already **in the Consensus corpus**, use [Chat with full text](/web/chat-with-full-text) instead.
</Info>

## Upload and start chatting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your PDF">
    Add the document you want to work with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions about it">
    Once it is uploaded, ask targeted questions and Consensus answers from the text of your document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Keep the conversation going to go deeper on methods, results, or any section that matters to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to ask

The same kinds of targeted questions that work for a corpus paper work here:

- "What question does this paper set out to answer?"
- "Summarize the methods in plain language."
- "What are the main results, and what are their limitations?"
- "What do the authors recommend for future work?"

<Warning>
  Answers are AI summaries of your document and can miss nuance. For anything high-stakes, confirm against the source text.
</Warning>

## Upload and chat vs chat with full text

Both give you a grounded conversation about one paper. The difference is whose document it is:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upload and chat" icon="file-arrow-up">
    You provide the document. Best for a **PDF of your own** that may not be in the corpus.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat with full text" icon="comments" href="/web/chat-with-full-text">
    Consensus provides the document. Best for a paper that is **already in the corpus**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
