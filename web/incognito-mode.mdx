---
title: "Incognito mode"
description: "Run searches without saving them to your history. Turn Incognito Mode on for sensitive or one-off searches."
---

When Incognito Mode is on, your searches are not saved to your [search history](/web/search-history). It is the privacy counterpart to history: use it for sensitive topics, or one-off searches you would rather not keep.

## Turn on Incognito Mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile menu">
    Click your profile icon in the bottom left of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Incognito Mode on">
    Switch **Incognito Mode** on. It stays on until you turn it off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search as usual">
    Run your searches normally. While Incognito Mode is on, nothing is added to your search history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can also turn Incognito Mode on or off from your **Settings**.
</Note>

## Turn it off

Toggle **Incognito Mode** off from your profile menu or your **Settings** to resume saving searches to your history. Searches you ran while it was on were never saved, so they will not appear in your history afterward.
