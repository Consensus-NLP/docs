Consensus works on every plan. What changes as you upgrade is how many papers each search draws on, which modes you can run, and how much you can search each month.

## Papers per search

Papers per search is the number of papers a single search reads and ranks. It grows with your plan.

| Plan | Papers per search | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anonymous (no account) | 3 | Ranked results without signing in |
| Free | 10 | Full abstracts on every result |
| Pro | 20 | Pro Search, the study-type filter, and takeaways |
| Deep | 20 (Deep review reads up to 50) | Deep review and takeaways |
| Teams | 20 | Team features |
| Enterprise | Custom | Advanced access |

[Deep review](/web/search-modes) is the exception to the per-search cap: for one comprehensive literature review it reads up to 50 papers, breaking your question into sub-questions and running many targeted searches behind the scenes.

<Note>
  Papers per search is separate from your monthly search volume, which also varies by plan. For current monthly limits and pricing, see the [pricing page](https://consensus.app/pricing).
</Note>

## What each plan unlocks

Every search mode is available to try, and the deeper modes and higher volumes scale with your plan. [Quick Search](/web/search-modes) is the default on every plan; **Pro Search** and **Deep review** power the more thorough modes, both driven by the Research Agent and both able to hold context across follow-up questions in [Threads](/web/threads).

For a full breakdown of what each mode does and when to use it, see [Search modes](/web/search-modes).
