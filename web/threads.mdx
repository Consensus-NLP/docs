Threads let you keep the conversation going. After a [Pro Search](/web/pro-search) or [Deep review](/web/deep-review), you can ask a follow-up question within the same search while keeping its context, instead of starting over and losing the thread of what you already found.

<Info>
  Threads are available in Pro Search and Deep review. Quick Search returns a ranked list on its own and does not carry a follow-up conversation.
</Info>

## When to use it

Use a follow-up when your next question builds on the last one:

- Drill into one of the papers or sub-questions the search surfaced.
- Narrow or broaden the scope without rewriting your whole prompt.
- Ask for a comparison, a counterpoint, or more detail on a specific finding.
- Redirect to a related question while keeping the same context.

## How it works

Because the follow-up stays in the same search, Consensus keeps the context of your original question and the papers already found. You ask in plain language, and the [Research Agent](/web/research-agent) responds in light of what came before, rather than treating your follow-up as a brand-new search.
