Filters let you narrow a search to the evidence that fits your question. Apply one or combine several to focus on the studies that matter, then clear them to widen the search again.

Every filter below has a counterpart in the Consensus API, so a search you refine by clicking maps directly to a programmatic request.

## Available filters

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Study type" icon="flask">
    Limit results to specific study designs, such as randomized controlled trials, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, or observational studies. Use it for clinical or evidence-based questions where the strength of the design matters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Publication year" icon="calendar">
    Restrict results to a date range. Use it when you want the most recent evidence, or when you need to focus on a particular period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Journal" icon="book">
    Limit results to specific journals. Use it when you follow particular venues, or want to track how one journal covers a topic.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Journal quality (SJR quartile)" icon="award">
    Filter by the journal's SCImago Journal Rank (SJR) quartile, from Q1 (highest) to Q4. Use it to prioritize papers published in higher-ranked journals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sample size" icon="users">
    Filter by the number of participants or observations in a study. Use it when you want findings backed by larger samples.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Domain (field of study)" icon="layer-group">
    Limit results to a field or discipline, such as medicine, psychology, or biology. Use it when a term spans several fields and you want results from just one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Country" icon="earth-americas">
    Narrow to research associated with a specific country. Use it when geography is central to your question, for example region-specific policy or populations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Open access" icon="lock-open">
    Limit results to papers that are free to read in full. Use it when you need the full text without a paywall.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Consensus offers these filters and more. Combine them to move from a broad scan to a focused set of studies in a few clicks.

## Filters across search modes

In [Quick Search](/web/quick-search) you set filters yourself, before or after you run a search. In Pro Search and Deep review, the Research Agent can also apply academic filters (such as study type, recency, journal quality, and sample size) for you, based on how you phrase your question. See [Search modes](/web/search-modes) to pick the right mode for your question.
