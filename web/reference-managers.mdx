When you are ready to cite the papers you found, take them out of Consensus. Export results and saved papers to your reference manager, or copy a formatted citation to paste straight into a manuscript.

## Export to a reference manager

Export Consensus results and saved papers to any of these reference managers:

- **EndNote**
- **Zotero**
- **Paperpile**

Export works for papers in your results and for papers you have saved to [My Library](/web/library), so you can send out a single paper or a set you have collected.

<Tip>
  Gather the papers into a [collection](/web/collections) first, then export the collection as a set instead of one paper at a time.
</Tip>

## Inline citation styles

For a citation you can drop directly into your writing, generate one in a standard style:

- **APA**
- **MLA**
- **Chicago**
- **Vancouver**

Every Consensus result is grounded in a real paper with a DOI, so both exported references and generated citations point back to the source.
