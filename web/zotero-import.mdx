---
title: "Import your Zotero library"
description: "Bring your existing Zotero library into Consensus so your references live alongside your searches."
---

Already keep your references in Zotero? Import your Zotero library into Consensus so the papers you have collected sit next to your searches, collections, and saved results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Zotero import" icon="file-import">
    Go to [My Library](/web/library) and choose to import from Zotero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Zotero account" icon="link">
    Authorize Consensus to access your Zotero library so it can read your references.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your references" icon="download">
    Consensus brings your Zotero library in, so your existing references are available alongside the rest of your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize what you imported" icon="folder-open">
    Group imported papers into [collections](/web/collections) by project, research question, or manuscript.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For the current, detailed import steps, see the [help center](https://help.consensus.app).
</Note>

## Going the other way

Import brings references into Consensus. To move papers out of Consensus and back into Zotero or another tool, see [export to reference managers](/web/reference-managers).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Export to reference managers" icon="file-export" href="/web/reference-managers">
    Send Consensus results and saved papers to EndNote, Zotero, or Paperpile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collections" icon="folder-open" href="/web/collections">
    Organize imported papers into collections.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
