---
title: "Collections"
description: "Create collections to group and organize saved papers by project, research question, or manuscript."
---

Collections keep your saved papers organized. Instead of one long list, sort papers into groups that match how you work: one collection per project, one per research question, or one per manuscript.

## Create a collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the papers you want to keep" icon="bookmark">
    Save papers from your results into [My Library](/web/library). Anything you save is available to add to a collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a collection" icon="folder-plus">
    Create a new collection and give it a name that matches the work, such as a project, a research question, or a manuscript you are writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add papers to it" icon="circle-plus">
    Add saved papers to the collection. A collection gathers the papers that belong together so you can find them as a set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work from a collection

Open a collection from My Library to see its papers together. A collection is a natural unit to build on: read through it, [export its papers to a reference manager](/web/reference-managers), or [share it](/web/sharing-collections) with a colleague.

<Tip>
  Name a collection for the work it supports, such as a specific review question or a manuscript section, so the right papers are easy to find when you come back to them.
</Tip>
