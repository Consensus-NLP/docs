The Research Agent is the engine that powers [Pro Search](/web/pro-search) and [Deep review](/web/deep-review). It is what turns your question into a plan, runs the searches, and reads the papers, so the summary you get is grounded in the evidence.

<Info>
  The Research Agent is not a separate mode you pick. You use it by running Pro Search or Deep review; the agent works behind the scenes in both.
</Info>

## What it does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understands your question">
    It handles messy prompts, precise questions, and Boolean-style queries alike, so you can ask in plain language or with operators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plans and runs sub-searches">
    It breaks the question down and runs multiple sub-searches rather than a single query.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uses research tools">
    It follows the evidence with tools such as citation crawling, DOI lookup, author search, and finding similar papers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Applies academic filters">
    It narrows to trustworthy evidence using [filters](/web/filters) such as study type, recency, journal quality, sample size, and population.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads and compares papers">
    It reads and compares the papers it finds to surface the findings that answer your question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you use it

You do not configure or launch the Research Agent directly. Run a deeper mode and it takes over from there:

- In **[Pro Search](/web/pro-search)**, it works across up to 20 papers and writes a connected summary.
- In **[Deep review](/web/deep-review)**, it breaks a complex question into sub-questions and reviews up to 50 papers into a structured report.
