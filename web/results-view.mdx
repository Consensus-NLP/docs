---
title: "Changing your results view"
description: "Switch how your search results are displayed so the layout matches your workflow, whether you are scanning broadly or focusing on a few papers."
---

You can change how your search results are displayed to suit your workflow. The results themselves do not change; only how they are laid out does, so you can pick whatever helps you work through them right now.

## Pick a view for the task at hand

Different tasks call for different layouts:

- **Scanning broadly**: when you are surveying a topic, a denser layout lets you move down the list quickly and spot the papers worth opening.
- **Focusing on a few papers**: when you have narrowed your search, a layout that gives each result more room makes the takeaway and details easier to read.

Change the view whenever your task changes. It affects only the display, so you can switch back and forth without rerunning your search.
