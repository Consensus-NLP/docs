Consensus saves your past searches to your account, so you can return to earlier questions without running them again.

## Revisit a past search

Your recent searches are kept with your account. Reopen one to return to that search and its results, then keep reading, adjust your filters, or ask a follow-up.

## Reuse your work

A past search is a starting point, not just a record:

- **Reopen it** to see the results again without running the search from scratch.
- **Run it again** with different filters or a different [search mode](/web/search-modes) when you want to adjust your approach.
- **Continue with follow-up questions** in Pro Search and Deep review, which keep context across a conversation.

## Incognito Mode bypasses history

While [Incognito Mode](/web/incognito-mode) is on, your searches are not saved to your history. Use it for sensitive or one-off searches you would rather not keep, then turn it off to resume saving.

<Note>
  Incognito Mode is the privacy counterpart to search history. Searches you run while it is on never enter your history and cannot be recovered later.
</Note>
