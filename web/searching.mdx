Every search runs over the same index of **200 million+ peer-reviewed papers**. How you search depends on what you need: a fast, ranked list of papers, or a synthesized answer that pulls from many searches for you.

## Search modes

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Quick Search" icon="bolt">
    Fast, ranked results for a single query. You get a list of the most relevant papers, each with its AI takeaway and metadata. Best for scanning the literature and finding specific papers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro and Deep research" icon="robot">
    A multi-step agent that plans and runs many searches, reads across the results, and synthesizes a cited answer. Best for open questions where you want the evidence pulled together for you.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Quick Search is what the [REST API](/api-reference/overview) returns: one query in, a ranked page of papers out. Pro and Deep research runs an agent loop on top of that. To reproduce the loop in your own product, orchestrate several API searches yourself, or see the [use cases](/use-cases/overview) for worked examples.

<Note>
  Which search modes you can use, and how many papers each search returns, depend on your plan. Compare plans on the [pricing page](https://consensus.app/pricing).
</Note>

## Run your first search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to consensus.app">
    Open [consensus.app](https://consensus.app) and sign in. You can also search without an account, with fewer papers per search and a reduced set of fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a research question">
    Type a specific question in plain language, such as `Does creatine improve muscle strength?`. Full questions rank better than bare keywords.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a search mode">
    Choose **Quick Search** for a ranked list, or **Pro** or **Deep** research for a synthesized, cited answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow with filters">
    Apply filters like study type, year, or open access to focus the results. The filters are covered below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Scan the ranked papers and their takeaways, and check the [Consensus Meter](/web/understanding-results) for yes/no questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Write a good research query

Consensus is built for natural-language research questions, so the way you phrase a query changes what you get back.

- **Ask a full question, not a bare keyword.** Consensus ranks best when it can read the intent behind your query.
- **Name the specifics.** A clear population, intervention, comparison, and outcome give ranking more to work with.
- **Stay neutral.** Leading questions bias what you notice, not what the evidence says. Ask what the research shows, not to confirm what you already believe.
- **Push constraints into filters.** Filter by year or study type instead of typing those limits into the question text.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask a clear question">
    - `Does intermittent fasting improve insulin sensitivity in adults with type 2 diabetes?`
    - `What are the cardiovascular risks of long-term NSAID use?`

    Each names who, what, and the outcome you care about, so ranking has something concrete to match.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Avoid vague or leading queries">
    - `fasting diabetes`
    - `prove NSAIDs are dangerous`

    Too broad or too leading. Add the population and outcome, and drop the assumed answer.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  For more on framing neutral, effective prompts, read the Consensus guide [Framing unbiased research prompts](https://consensus.app/home/resources/ask-it-right/).
</Tip>

## Filters

Filters narrow a search to the studies that matter for your question. They map directly onto the [API filters](/api-reference/filters), so a query you refine in the web app translates cleanly to code.

| Filter | What it does | API parameter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Study type | Restrict to designs like RCT, meta-analysis, systematic review, or cohort study | `study_types` |
| Year | Limit to a publication-year range | `year_min`, `year_max` |
| Journal | Prefer specific journals, or restrict by publisher | `journal_name`, `publisher_name` |
| Open access | Show only papers with a free full-text version | `open_access` |
| Sample size | Require a minimum study sample size | `sample_size_min` |
| SJR quartile | Limit to higher-quartile journals (Q1 is the top quartile) | `sjr_min`, `sjr_max` |
| Domain | Restrict to a field such as medicine, biology, or computer science | `domain` |
| Country | Restrict to studies from specific countries | `country` |

<Tip>
  See the [filter reference](/api-reference/filters) for every filter, its exact values, and how to combine them. The same universe of filters is available in the web app and the API.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="Good to know before you search">
  - **Peer-reviewed by default.** Consensus focuses on peer-reviewed research and excludes preprints unless you opt in to include them.
  - **English-first.** The index and AI summaries work best with English-language queries and papers.
  - **Fresh papers can lag.** Newly published work may take some time to be indexed and appear in results.
</Accordion>
