Each use case below pairs a short problem statement with a runnable recipe against [`GET /v1/search`](/api-reference/endpoints/search). Copy the code, swap in your query, and adapt from there. Every workflow also runs from an LLM host through the [Consensus MCP server](/mcp/overview).

## Workflows

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Research synthesis" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/use-cases/research-synthesis">
    Turn a question into a cited, defensible answer built from peer-reviewed papers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Literature review automation" icon="list-check" href="/use-cases/literature-review">
    Gather across many queries, deduplicate, screen by filters, and export to BibTeX or CSV.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fact-checking" icon="scale-balanced" href="/use-cases/fact-checking">
    Check a claim against supporting and refuting evidence, then return a verdict with citations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paper discovery" icon="compass" href="/use-cases/paper-discovery">
    Surface the most relevant papers on a topic, then widen or narrow with filters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cross-study comparison" icon="chart-column" href="/use-cases/cross-study-comparison">
    Compare outcomes across studies, grouped by design, population, and geography.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
